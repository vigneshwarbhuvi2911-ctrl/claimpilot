# Private Car Package Policy — Policy Wording (Demo)

Product code: MOTOR_PC. This is a synthetic policy wording created for a demonstration project.
It is not the wording of any real insurer.

## M1. Cover for own damage
The Company will indemnify the insured against loss of or damage to the insured vehicle caused by
accidental external means, fire, explosion, flood, storm or theft. Cover is limited to the Insured
Declared Value (IDV) shown in the schedule.

## M2. Period of insurance
The Company is liable only for loss or damage that occurs during the period of insurance shown in the
schedule. Loss or damage occurring before the start date or after the end date is not covered.

## M3. Notice of claim
The insured must notify the Company of any loss within 7 days of the incident. Claims notified later
may be considered on merit; the Company may ask for the reason for the delay before deciding.

## M4. Compulsory deductible
A compulsory deductible of Rs 1,000 applies to every own-damage claim and is deducted from the
assessed loss.

## M5. Depreciation on parts
Depreciation is deducted from the cost of parts replaced, as follows:
- Rubber, nylon and plastic parts: 50%
- Glass parts: nil
- Metal parts, by age of the vehicle on the date of loss:
  - not exceeding 6 months: nil
  - exceeding 6 months but not exceeding 1 year: 5%
  - exceeding 1 year but not exceeding 2 years: 10%
  - exceeding 2 years but not exceeding 3 years: 15%
  - exceeding 3 years but not exceeding 4 years: 25%
  - exceeding 4 years but not exceeding 5 years: 35%
  - exceeding 5 years: 40%
Labour charges are paid without depreciation.

## M6. General exclusions
The Company is not liable for:
- (a) loss or damage while the vehicle is driven by a person under the influence of alcohol or drugs;
- (b) loss or damage while the vehicle is driven by a person who does not hold a valid driving licence;
- (c) mechanical or electrical breakdown, wear and tear, or depreciation of the vehicle;
- (d) loss or damage while the vehicle is used for hire or reward, as this is a private car policy.

## M7. Theft
In the case of theft where the vehicle is not recovered, the Company will pay the IDV less the
compulsory deductible. A First Information Report (FIR) lodged with the police is mandatory.

## M8. Documents required
Every claim must include the claim form, a copy of the driver's licence and the repair estimate
(not required for unrecovered theft). Theft claims must also include the FIR.
