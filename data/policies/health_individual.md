# Individual Health Insurance Policy — Policy Wording (Demo)

Product code: HEALTH_IND. This is a synthetic policy wording created for a demonstration project.
It is not the wording of any real insurer.

## H1. Cover for hospitalisation
The Company will pay the reasonable and customary medical expenses incurred for in-patient
hospitalisation of at least 24 consecutive hours, up to the available sum insured.

## H2. Period of insurance
Only hospitalisation that begins during the current period of insurance is covered.

## H3. Waiting periods
- (a) Initial waiting period: expenses for any illness contracted within 30 days of the first policy
  start date are not covered. This does not apply to hospitalisation caused by an accident.
- (b) Pre-existing diseases: expenses related to a pre-existing disease declared or detected at
  proposal are covered only after 36 months of continuous cover from the first policy start date.
- (c) Specified diseases and procedures — cataract, hernia and joint replacement — are covered only
  after 24 months of continuous cover from the first policy start date.

## H4. Room rent limit and proportionate deduction
Room rent is payable up to 1% of the sum insured per day. If the insured stays in a room with a
higher rent, the excess room rent is not payable, and the room-related charges (nursing charges,
doctor's fees and investigations) are paid in the same proportion as the eligible room rent bears to
the actual room rent. Medicines are not subject to proportionate deduction.

## H5. Co-payment
For an insured person aged 60 years or more on the date of admission, a co-payment of 10% applies
to every admissible claim, after all other deductions.

## H6. Non-payable items
Consumables such as gloves, masks, PPE kits and toiletries, and other non-medical items, are not
payable.

## H7. Exclusions
The Company is not liable for:
- (a) cosmetic or aesthetic treatment, unless needed because of an accident;
- (b) intentional self-injury;
- (c) treatment for alcohol or drug abuse or any condition arising from it.

## H8. Sum insured
The total of all claims paid in a policy year cannot exceed the sum insured. Any claim is limited to
the sum insured still available on the date of admission.

## H9. Notice of claim
For reimbursement claims, documents must be submitted within 30 days of discharge. Late submission
may be considered on merit.

## H10. Documents required
Every claim must include the claim form, the discharge summary and the final hospital bill with an
itemised breakup.
